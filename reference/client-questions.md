# When a client asks you something

You are allowed to not know. In week one, your job is to take the question down and pass it to the right person.

## The line to say

> "That's an important one, and I want you to get it right from the right person. I'm new, so I'll take your question to my colleague."

Do not promise when the answer will come unless the owner of the question has told you.

## Then
1. Write the question down in the client's own words, in the company's tools.
2. Pass it to the owner the same day. Find them in `who-to-ask.md`.

## Phrases never to use
- "You'll own it outright."
- "It's a 90-year lease."
- "It's guaranteed to renew."
- "It's totally safe from flooding."
- "That's how everyone does it here."
