# Property words

For your own understanding. Do not use this page to answer a client. Rules change, and each property is different; Ingrid takes client questions to the lawyer.

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Chanote | The strongest kind of Thai land title |
| Nor Sor 3 Gor | A land document ranked below a Chanote. Not the same thing |
| Land Office | The government office where transfers and long leases are registered |
| Leasehold | The right to use property for a set time. It is not ownership of the land |
| Registered lease | A lease recorded at the Land Office. A lease longer than three years needs this to be enforceable past three years. The longest term is 30 years |
| "30+30+30" | A sales phrase for a long lease with promised renewals. Do not repeat it. Law firms report that Thailand's Supreme Court has ruled pre-agreed renewals of this kind void |
| Foreign land ownership | Foreigners generally cannot own land in Thailand. There are narrow exceptions, which are the lawyer's subject |
| Foreign quota | In a condo building, the share foreigners may own outright is capped. The figure commonly given is 49% of the floor area. Whether one building has room must be checked |
| FET form | A bank record of money brought in from abroad. It matters for some foreign purchases, so a buyer's papers should never be thrown away |
| Juristic person (condo) | The body that manages a condo building |
| Rai | A land measure: 1,600 square metres |
| Wah (square wah) | A land measure: 4 square metres |
