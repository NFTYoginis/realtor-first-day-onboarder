# Our three areas

General orientation for a new agent. Not for quoting to clients. No prices are kept here.

| Area | Feel | Check before saying anything to anyone |
|---|---|---|
| **Nang Thong** | The busiest, most walkable part: restaurants, shops, dive shops | Whether reaching the beach means crossing the main road; evening noise |
| **Bang Niang** | Quieter, still walkable, has a night market and many long-stay Europeans. Our office is here | Drainage and flood history on inland side lanes, property by property |
| **Khuk Khak** | North of Bang Niang. Resorts toward the beach, village feel inland; more spread out | For inland homes: road access, water supply, distance to shops |

The main road is Highway 4 (Phetkasem). High season is roughly November to April; the rainy low season is roughly May to October.

This folder does not hold market days or times, travel times, school details or business recommendations.
