# First day

Day one is for settling in. You are not expected to know the market or sell anything.

## Step 1: Arrive (9:00)
- Come to the office in Bang Niang for 9:00.
- Find Noi. If Noi is not there, ask whoever is in to tell Noi or Ingrid you have arrived.
- Bring your passport and a charged phone.

## Step 2: Paperwork check with Noi (before any client contact)
- Ask Noi: "What am I cleared to do today?"
- Until Noi has told you, do not do any work with or for a client. This folder does not say what your paperwork allows.

## Step 3: Get set up with Noi
- Team contact list, saved to your phone
- LINE group invite
- Work email, shared drive and calendar access
- The fire exit and the first aid kit, shown to you in person

## Step 4: Meet Marc (late morning)
- Marc is your shadow lead this week.
- Ask: "What is on your calendar this week that I can watch?"

## Step 5: Read three things (after lunch)
- `office-rules.md`
- `client-questions.md`
- `safety.md`

## Step 6: Learn the tools (afternoon)
- Open the team calendar and find today.
- Open the listings sheet. Look, do not edit.

## Step 7: Before you leave
- Ask Marc what time to come in tomorrow and where to meet.
- If you were handed a key or a document, return it and check the entry with Noi.
- Write down the questions you did not get answered, with a name next to each.

## Can wait until later this week
Area knowledge, property words, shadowing viewings, how listings are put together.

## Not for week one
Running a viewing alone, editing the listings sheet, answering client questions yourself.
