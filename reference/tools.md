# Tools and logins

No passwords, codes or phone numbers are stored here. Noi gives access in person.

| Tool | What it is for | In week one |
|---|---|---|
| Team contact list | Everyone's numbers | Save it to your phone on day one |
| LINE group | Quick team messages | Read and reply. No client private details in the group |
| Work email | Anything that needs a record | Use only the work address for company matters |
| Team calendar | Viewings, who is booked when | Read. Ask Marc before adding anything |
| Shared drive | Company documents | Open what you are pointed to |
| Listings sheet | The current list of properties and their details | Look only. Praew makes changes |
| Key register (paper, at the key cabinet) | Who has which key | Run by Noi |

**Which to open first:** the team calendar.

If a login does not work, tell Noi. Do not borrow a colleague's login.
