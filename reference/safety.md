# Safety

## Emergency numbers in Thailand
Checked against public government travel and tourism pages on 27 November 2026.

| Need | Number |
|---|---|
| Ambulance, medical emergency | **1669** |
| Police | **191** |
| Fire | **199** |
| Tourist Police (English-speaking help) | **1155** |

## Someone is hurt or very unwell
1. Call **1669** and do what the operator tells you.
2. Get a colleague. Stay with the person.
3. After they are safe, tell Noi and Ingrid.

## Fire or smoke
1. Get out and warn others.
2. Call **199** from outside.
3. Do not go back in for keys, laptops or files.

## Tsunami warning, siren, or a strong or long earthquake near the coast
1. Move away from the sea to higher ground now. Follow official warnings and posted evacuation signs.
2. Do not wait for a colleague's permission, and do not go to look at the water.
3. Take any client with you.
4. Message the team once you are safe.

This folder does not name a meeting point or route. Ask Noi on day one.

## Flooding
Do not drive or walk into floodwater. Cancel or delay and tell Marc.

## You feel unsafe with a person
Leave. You do not need to explain or finish the viewing. If you are in danger call **191**. Then tell Ingrid.

## Heat
If you feel faint, stop, get into shade and tell the person you are with. If someone collapses or is confused, call **1669**.
