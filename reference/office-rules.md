# Office rules

These are Lantern Cove's own rules.

## Hours and presence
- The office day is 9:00 to 18:00. Your own days and days off are agreed with Noi.
- Running late or sick: message Noi directly before 9:00.
- Lunch is flexible. Tell someone in the office when you step out.

## What to wear
- Smart casual, with a collar or equivalent. Closed or strapped shoes you can slip off, since many homes are shoes-off.
- No beachwear at viewings.

## Keys
- Property keys live in the office key cabinet. Noi runs it.
- Every key is signed out and signed back in on the key register, the same day.
- No key goes home overnight.
- In week one you do not take a key out on your own.
- Never label a key with an address.

## Money
- A junior never accepts money from anyone: no cash, no transfer to a personal account, no "holding it until tomorrow".
- If someone tries to hand you money, say you cannot take it and get Ingrid.

## Clients
- In week one you are with a colleague whenever a client is present.
- Do not give a price opinion, discuss a discount, or promise anything.
- Do not give your own answer to a legal, tax, ownership, flood or tsunami question. See `client-questions.md`.
- Do not sign anything for the company or for a client.

## Cars
- Do not carry a client in your own car or on your motorbike unless Noi has cleared it.

## Privacy
- Client details stay in the company's tools. No copies in your personal notes, photo roll or personal accounts.
- Do not put client details into this onboarding chat or any other AI tool.
- No photos of clients, their documents or the inside of an owner's home for your own use.

## Social media and press
- Do not post about a client, a property or a deal. Praew handles company posts.

## 26 December
- This is the anniversary of the 2004 tsunami. The team runs no promotions around that date.
